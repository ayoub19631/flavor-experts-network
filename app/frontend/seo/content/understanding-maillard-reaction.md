The **Maillard reaction** is one of the most important chemical processes in flavor science. When reducing sugars react with amino acids under heat, they produce hundreds of volatile compounds that give roasted, caramelized, and savory characteristics to foods.

## Why It Matters for Flavorists

Flavor chemists leverage Maillard chemistry to replicate authentic cooking notes in processed foods. Understanding temperature, moisture, and pH helps predict which pyrazines, furans, and thiazoles will dominate a profile.

## Practical Applications

- **Savory snacks**: Roasted nut and meaty notes
- **Beverages**: Coffee, malt, and cocoa depth
- **Bakery**: Crust aroma and browned butter facets

Controlling precursor ratios and processing conditions allows R&D teams to achieve consistent sensory targets while meeting clean-label expectations.
